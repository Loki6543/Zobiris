# Rotation policy revision — October 2, 2026

User-approved thresholds supersede the earlier whitepaper values:

- Seven-day positive market breadth: at least 50% (previously 55%).
- Asset 30-day return: −35% through +45%, inclusive.
- Asset 90-day return: −70% through +55%, inclusive.

BTC 30/90-day positivity, minimum 10 comparable assets, 45% 30-day market breadth, positive 7-day recovery up to 12%, and 24-hour gain no greater than 8% remain required. All research, Kraken execution, account, confidence, and capital gates still apply.

The screen now admits positive longer-term returns; qualification no longer means the coin lost value over both longer windows. Existing lag-oriented ranking weights and pullback pricing remain unchanged. This is a user-selected policy revision, not a backtested performance claim.
