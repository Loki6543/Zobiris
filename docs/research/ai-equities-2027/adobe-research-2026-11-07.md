## 1. The business today (Q3 FY26)

**Growth:**
- Revenue $6.76bn, +13% y/y (+12% constant currency). This includes the Semrush acquisition; Experience Cloud grows low-teens excluding Semrush (Goldman).
- Total ARR $27.50bn. The FY26 target for ending ARR growth is 10.2%.
- RPO $22.16bn.

**Profitability:**
- Non-GAAP operating margin about 45%. GAAP operating income $2.35bn (about 35% margin).
- FY26 guidance: revenue $26.58–26.63bn; non-GAAP EPS $24.45–24.50 (P/E about 9.8 at $239); GAAP EPS about $18.15 (P/E about 13).

**AI and users:**
- AI-first ARR above $650M, growing more than 150%. That is only about 2.4% of total ARR.
- Monthly active users above 1bn (+20%). Creative freemium users 100M (+70%).

**Cash** (trailing four quarters):
- Operating cash flow $10.81bn, capex $0.21bn, FCF $10.59bn.
- Stock compensation $2.07bn, so FCF minus stock compensation is $8.52bn.
- Buybacks $9.29bn (88% of FCF); about 9.5M shares retired in Q3. Diluted shares are guided to about 389M in Q4.
- Net debt about $0.7bn (debt $6.36bn, cash and short-term investments $5.64bn).

## 2. Why it fell

- **AI competition.** Generative tools "democratised" creative skills (William Blair).
  - Canva has about $4bn ARR, growing more than 30%; Figma about $1.2bn, growing about 40%.
  - AI natives: Midjourney, Runway, Stability. Platforms: Google, OpenAI, Apple.
- **Leadership change.** Narayen's exit was announced in March; Anil Chakravarthy becomes CEO on 1 Dec 2026. The creative head (Wadhwani) is leaving, and the CFO is interim.
- **Q3 reaction.** The quarter beat and full-year targets were raised, but the shares fell on Q4 guidance slightly below consensus.
- **Analysts.** Goldman Sachs: Sell. William Blair: Market Perform ("about 9x FCF … range-bound").

## 5. Verdict: conditional value candidate, small

**For:**
- The price assumes cash flow shrinking about 1% a year, while the business still grows about 10% (ARR) with 45% margins.
- Buybacks retire about 7–8% of shares a year.
- Equity value is self-funded, with low net debt.

**Against:**
- A real competitive threat.
- A leadership transition.
- AI revenue still small.
- The trend has just turned down.

**Catalyst.** Q4 FY26 results and FY27 guidance, the new CEO's first, are due in mid-December (date unconfirmed).

**Portfolio fit.** ADBE shares the "AI-disrupted seat-based software" factor with CRM. Count ADBE and CRM together.

**Qualification test after the December results:**
- FY27 ARR growth guided at 9% or more.
- Non-GAAP margin at about 44% or above.
- No second weekly close below the 40-week average before then.
