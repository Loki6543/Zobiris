# Diversifier Recession Comparison (29 Oct 2026)

**Data:** Yahoo weekly adjusted closes, with dividends reinvested. Weekly closes understate intraweek lows, so the true drawdowns were somewhat deeper.

**How to read it:** DD is the maximum peak-to-trough drawdown within the window. R is the return from the first to the last week of the window.

| Window | CB | BRK-B | IDV | SCHD | SPY |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Financial crisis, Oct 2007 to Dec 2009 | DD −50 / R −15 | DD −53 / R −18 | **DD −69 / R −31** | n/a (launched 2011) | DD −55 / R −25 |
| Late-2018 selloff, Sep 2018 to Mar 2019 | DD −12 / R +4 | DD −13 / R −6 | DD −13 / R 0 | DD −15 / R +1 | DD −17 / R 0 |
| 2020 (Covid crash) | DD −39 / R +5 | **DD −26** / R +2 | DD −41 / R −6 | DD −31 / R +15 | DD −32 / R +17 |
| 2022 (rate hikes) | **DD −17 / R +11** | DD −26 / R −3 | DD −28 / R −9 | DD −17 / R −4 | DD −22 / R −17 |
| 2025 | DD −10 / R +21 | DD −14 / R +12 | DD −7 / **R +56** | DD −11 / R +7 | DD −17 / R +19 |
| Oct 2011 to Oct 2026, total return | +554% | +576% | +198% | +551% | +680% |

**Reading:**
- **IDV fell the most in every broad crisis** and had the weakest long-run return. Its one standout year was 2025.
- **Chubb held up best in the 2022 rate-hike year**, the regime most like today's.
- **Berkshire held up best in the 2020 crash.**

**Decision (owner's plan, research only):** replace IDV with Chubb plus Berkshire as the diversifiers.
