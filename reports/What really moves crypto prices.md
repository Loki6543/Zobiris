# Supply, beta and exchange notices move altcoins

For altcoins outside the top 100, the drivers with the strongest historical evidence are not the ones crypto media covers most. The ranking runs as follows. **Market/BTC beta** sets the direction and amplitude of most moves. **Supply events** come next: team unlocks, insider and market-maker deposits to exchanges, and low float. **Exchange notices** follow, with delistings and Monitoring Tags the most durable and Korean listings the sharpest but shortest-lived. **Hacks** and **SEC actions naming a specific token** are rare but cause large, lasting damage. **Leverage crowding** does not tell you direction, but it sets how big a crash will be. Everything usually marketed as a catalyst is mostly noise or sell-the-news once measured: partnerships, mainnets, airdrops, most buybacks, ETF headlines for small caps, and macro direction. Social attention is real but fleeting. It is a short continuation signal on organic, abnormal mentions, and at extremes it works as a contrarian exit signal. Fundamentals like TVL show no measurable return predictability, and only user adoption per dollar of value has modest academic support. Lead times vary widely. Unlocks are known weeks to months ahead. Hacks and listings leak on-chain or into prices hours to days before publication. Announcement pops are mostly over within the first hour, so a server-polled engine like Zobiris should treat notices as risk filters and fade or confirm triggers, not as races to win. Much of the industry evidence uses raw rather than abnormal returns, often without control groups, and 2025 was a down-market. The direction of the findings is well supported; the exact magnitudes deserve skepticism.

## BTC beta frames everything, but small caps live on idiosyncratic shocks

A single market factor closely tied to Bitcoin dominates large-cap crypto. Among majors, the first principal component explained about **37% of return variance in the earliest year and about 82% by 2020** ([arXiv 2009.14561](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.14561)). A 2025 study puts **79% of market variation** in the first component, led by BTC, ETH and stETH ([REMEF](https://www.remef.org.mx/index.php/remef/article/download/1094/997)). The broad universe looks different. Starting from the top 1,000 assets, the first ten factors explained only **about 60%** of return variability ([Talos](https://talos.com/insights/data-driven-crypto-risk-factors)). Across a wider cross-section, the first ten components explained at most about 30% ([CERGE-EI WP710](https://ei.cerge-ei.cz/pdf/wp/Wp710.pdf)). The practical reading: BTC beta is the first thing to model for any altcoin, but the further down the cap table you go, the more of the variance is a token-specific shock. Those shocks are unlocks, listings, market-maker behaviour and pumps.

The cross-section of expected returns is explained by three factors, market, size and momentum. Liu, Tsyvinski and Wu tested 25 candidate characteristics on coins above $1M market cap. Every profitable long-short strategy was absorbed by those three factors ([NBER w25882](https://www.nber.org/papers/w25882.pdf)). Illiquidity carries a premium as well ([IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/wly/ijfiec/v28y2023i1p472-492.html)). That premium is partly payment for crash risk. On October 10–11, 2025 a tariff headline set off **more than $19B of liquidations in 24 hours**, about $16.7B of it longs. Majors fell 10–15% while some altcoins fell more than 99% intraday ([Coin360](https://coin360.com/news/crypto-market-liquidation-2025-trump-tariffs); [Coin Metrics](https://coinmetrics.io/?p=27655)). Open interest dropped 22% in under 12 hours ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/crypto-liquidations-nearly-triple-overheating-leverage)). The damage lasted. Average 1% depth across ETH, XRP, SOL, SUI and LINK fell from about $2.6M to $1.7M, and combined volume of six major altcoins fell 60% ([Sherwood News](https://sherwood.news/crypto/altcoin-trading-activity-has-plummeted-60-percent-since-october-liquidation-event/)). Before the cascade, open interest in major altcoins had climbed **69% to $44B**, with 12 straight days of 2σ increases ([CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/altcoin-leverage-balloons-to-44-billion-setting-up-whipsaw-volatility/)). Leverage told you the crash would be big, not when it would come. Glassnode itself warns that funding and liquidation series lag ([Glassnode](https://insights.glassnode.com/leverage-position-openings-and-closures)). Funding and open interest should therefore scale position size and stop distance, not trigger entries.

Macro works the same way, as a volatility event passed through beta. Across 41 FOMC statements from 2021 to 2026, mean absolute return in the first hour roughly doubled, **from 0.66% to 1.25% for BTC**, and volume rose 2.5x ([IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v101y2026ics1544612326006021.html)). The initial FOMC move tends to **reverse within about four hours** ([SSRN 4478184](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4478184)). The NY Fed found BTC largely orthogonal to macro surprises over 2017–2022. CPI was the only release significant at 5% ([NY Fed SR1052](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr1052.pdf?sc_lang=en)). No study measures macro responses for small caps directly.

## Supply events are the most reliable negative signal, with the longest lead time

Token unlocks are the most consistently negative scheduled event in the evidence. Keyrock's study of **16,000+ unlocks** finds 90% create negative price pressure, with larger unlocks hitting up to 2.4x harder. **Team unlocks are worst at about −25%**, with a roughly linear decline starting more than 30 days before the date. Investor unlocks are mild because investors pre-hedge, and ecosystem unlocks average roughly flat to +1% ([Keyrock](https://keyrock.com/from-locked-to-liquidity-what-16000-token-unlocks-teach-us/); [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/keyrock-research-token-unlocks/)). Keyrock reports raw returns, so bear-market drift inflates the 90%. A smaller BTC-relative test corrects for that. Of 52 major unlocks of Binance-listed tokens over 2023–2025, **88.5% underperformed within 72 hours, with a mean of −17%**. Unlock size was an independent driver, and the effect did not depend on BTC regime ([SSRN 6632838](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6632838)). There is a counterweight. In The Tie's sample, 95.6% of 12,350 unlocks were smaller than one day's volume ([Streamflow](https://streamflow.finance/blog/do-token-unlocks-cause-price-drops)). The damage therefore concentrates in a minority of large team and insider unlocks, and those are what the engine should filter for.

The structural overhang explains why "good use case" coins stagnate. In 2024, new launches averaged **about 13% circulating supply and MC/FDV of about 12%**. Binance Research counted roughly **$155B of unlocks due through 2030** ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/binance-urges-small-medium-projects-high-fdv-trend); [ForkLog](https://forklog.com/en/binance-highlights-155-billion-token-unlock-overhang/)). Insider and market-maker flows produced the starkest single-token moves of the era. Movement's market maker sold about 66M MOVE into thin books the day after listing and netted about $38M ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/347931/binance-move-market-maker-movement-38-million-usdt-buyback-program)). Mantra OM lost about 90% within hours after a 3.9M OM deposit to OKX from a team-linked wallet, amid allegations that insiders controlled more than 90% of supply ([ForkLog](https://forklog.com/en/insider-collusion-blamed-for-mantra-token-crash/)). Generic "whale accumulation" signals are another matter. CryptoQuant's Exchange Whale Ratio thresholds have shifted over time, its posts report no sample sizes, and its founder concedes that on-chain data cannot see ETF and OTC buying ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/bitcoin-whales-hint-80k-market-rebound-binance-inflows-cool)). Nansen's "Smart Money" labels are assigned retroactively from past PnL ([Eco](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14800361-what-is-nansen-onchain-analytics-and-smart-money-explained)). The pattern is consistent. Whale signals about supply hitting exchanges are actionable. Whale signals about who is "smart" are unproven.

## Exchange notices move prices hardest, but mostly within the first hour

Listings produce a real but short-lived pop that has shrunk over time. In 2017–2019 samples, day-0 abnormal returns were about **+5.7–6.5%**, with **+5% leaking in over the three days before** and reversal starting at day +2 ([BRL working paper](https://www.blockchainresearchlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Exploring-Market-Reactions-to-Exchange-Listings-of-Cryptocurrencies-BRL-working-paper3.pdf); [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10203-021-00323-0)). For 2024 listings, RockawayX used a difference-in-differences design. Binance tokens pumped for a few days, then **sold back to zero gain within 30 days**. Bybit showed pre-listing run-ups, and MEXC small caps drifted down ([RockawayX](https://www.rockawayx.com/insights/bullish-or-bearish-what-really-happens-when-tokens-list-on-a-cex)). Over longer holds the result is a wipeout. Buyers at the first-day close of 2025 listings on Upbit, Bithumb and Binance lost **about 69–72%** by March 2026, and listing on several exchanges gave no protection ([HTX](https://www.htx.com/news/2025-korean-cex-listing-review-investing-in-new-tokens-70-lo-9ye26cVT/?+Analysis=&category=Research+&categoryTitle=Indepth+Research)). That figure is raw and covers a bad year, but the sign is unambiguous.

The largest dataset measured from official publish times covers 3,797 coin-events from 2022 to 2026. It ranks notices by median move. **Binance delistings: −12.4% at 1h, −15.5% at 24h, −22.7% at 7d.** Upbit spot listings: +13.9% at 1h, decaying to +7.2% at 24h and +1.9% at 7d. Binance spot listings: just +1.3% at 24h. Upbit caution tags: −3.1% ([CoinSight](https://getcoinsight.com/en/research/exchange-announcement-study)). Binance Monitoring Tags show a median 24h low of **−15.8%**, with the trough arriving about **3.9 hours** after detection ([cryptolisting.ws](https://cryptolisting.ws/listing-price-reaction/)). The asymmetry matters for a slow engine. The same study warns that a fast listing "can move tens of percent before any feed shows it" ([CoinSight](https://getcoinsight.com/en/research/new-listing-pump-by-exchange)). Delisting and tag drift, by contrast, keeps going for hours to days, so it can still be captured, or at least avoided.

Hacks and regulatory actions aimed at a token are the other high-impact events. DeFi hacks cut the protocol's governance token by **about −27% over 24 hours**, and abnormal returns are already significantly negative hours before the public announcement, because on-chain watchers trade first ([NY Fed SR1153](https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr1153.pdf)). When the SEC names a token as a security, that token's CAR deepens to **−17.2% over 30 days** without reversing, and illiquid assets fare worse ([Saggu via SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/ctf-input-arte-2025-02-19.pdf)). Broad enforcement actions are different: across 266 cases they lift BTC and leave other assets flat ([SSRN 6900460](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/6900460.pdf?abstractid=6900460&mirid=1)). Spot ETF approvals produced positive abnormal returns for BTC, ETH and LTC ([IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/quaeco/v102y2025ics106297692500047x.html)), but there is no evidence they lifted tokens outside the top 100.

## Most "catalysts" are noise, and attention decays within days

The scheduled-good-news category fails the measurement test. **88% of 2024 airdrop tokens declined** within months ([Keyrock](https://keyrock.com/airdrops-in-the-barren-desert/)), though 2025 was a more even 15 up, 15 down ([OAK Research](https://oakresearch.io/en/analyses/investigations/airdrops-2025-end-or-renewal)). Of ten buyback-and-burn announcements, only OKB, with a one-time ~$3B burn, and AAVE clearly beat BTC at 30 days. The UNI fee switch, the JUP burn and HYPE's buyback all lagged, and HYPE supply still grows about 47% a year ([Tokenomist](https://tokenomist.ai/research/buyback-and-burn-explained-what-they-are-who-is-doing-them-and-whether-they-actually-work)). Hard forks show no minute-level return effect. When the forked coin is distributed, the parent falls 12.3% over five days ([Economics Letters](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0165176525005099)). Predictability is the common thread. About **60% of predictable events show significant pre-event abnormal returns, versus about 11% of unpredictable ones** ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1544612326009293)). Partnerships have no crypto event study at all. For listed stocks, corporate blockchain announcements yielded only **+0.30%** on day 0, which then partly reversed ([POMS](https://sage.cnpereading.com/doi/10.1111/poms.13609)). Partnerships stay unquantified and should carry zero weight.

Attention is the most misunderstood driver. Investor-attention proxies do forecast crypto returns ([NBER w24877](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w24877/w24877.pdf)). Abnormal Twitter attention, measured as log posts minus their 30-day mean, predicts **same-day and next-day** returns. That predictability comes from investors' ticker-tweets, not from project accounts ([CFR WP 25-02](https://cfr-cologne.de/download/workingpaper/cfr-25-02.pdf)). Causality also runs the other way. One multi-platform index found that returns drive attention more than attention drives returns ([Bilkent](https://repository.bilkent.edu.tr/items/475a58c7-f85a-457c-8cb9-a64eb02583da)). At extremes the signal flips. Santiment reports an average 8.2% drop over 12 days after a coin enters its top-3 trending list. That claim comes from a vendor and has not been replicated ([Santiment](https://insights.santiment.net/read/want-to-know-when-the-pump-is-over-skip-this-study-at-your-own-risk-santiment-weekly-brief-5849?_wc=1)). Bots contaminate the data. More than 56% of accounts sharing Telegram or Discord invites were bots or suspended, and 93% of their links pointed to pump channels ([arXiv 2001.10289](https://ar5iv.arxiv.org/html/2001.10289)). Coins with more bot posts earned lower long-run returns ([Blockworks](https://blockworks.com/news/crypto-twitter-bots-returns)). Coordinated pumps last minutes and reverse almost completely. They can be detected within 25 seconds with 94.5% F1 ([arXiv 2105.00733](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.00733v2)). Fewer than 1% of Pump.fun memecoins ever graduate to a DEX ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/pump-fun-memecoins-are-dying-at-record-rates-less-than-1-survive)).

Fundamentals fare little better as timing signals. TVL/market cap does not predict returns in three separate tests ([arXiv 2204.00251](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.00251v1); [HAL](https://hal-cstb.archives-ouvertes.fr/LJK-MAD-CASC/hal-04507930v1); [Algorand](https://algorand.co/blog/the-surprising-irrelevance-of-total-value-locked-tvl-on-cryptocurrency-returns)). Adoption is the exception. High active-addresses-to-network-value coins earn **2.1 percentage points more per week** than size-matched peers ([UNISG](https://alexandria.unisg.ch/handle/20.500.14171/111544)), and new-user growth explains about 8% of return variation ([arXiv 2510.14435](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.14435)). Evidence for GitHub activity is weak or comes from vendors ([CryptoScores](https://www.cryptoscores.com/blog/fundamental-signals-cryptocurrency-performance)). Fees and revenue have only practitioner models behind them ([Greenfield](https://greenfieldcapital.com/?p=4397)). Fundamentals belong in a slow quality and survival filter, not in the trigger.

| 5 | SEC naming a token | Moderate | −17% over 30d, no recovery | Days; pre-event volume drop | Exit filter |

## Zobiris should wire supply and notice feeds before any sentiment

The cheapest high-value additions are free and need no key. Poll the **Binance bapi announcement endpoint** (catalogs 48, 49, 50, 157, 161, with a browser User-Agent and backoff) and the **Upbit api-manager notices** endpoint ([fabius8/binanceAlert](https://github.com/fabius8/binanceAlert/blob/main/binanceAlert.py); [paragraph.com/@dfarm](https://paragraph.com/@dfarm/2-upbit)). Parse them into listing, delisting, Monitoring Tag and caution events. Add **Hyperliquid `metaAndAssetCtxs`**, a single POST returning funding and open interest for every perp ([Hyperliquid docs](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/info-endpoint/perpetuals)). Use it to compute cross-venue funding extremes and open-interest change z-scores in-house rather than paying Coinglass. Snapshot **CoinGecko `/search/trending`** about every 10 minutes on the free Demo key. At 144 calls a day that fits inside the 10k monthly quota ([CoinGecko](https://docs.coingecko.com/demo/reference/trending-search)). Use DefiLlama's free endpoints for TVL and price context only, not as a return signal. The one paid feed worth buying is an unlock calendar. **Tokenomist Pro ($69–99/mo) or CryptoRank Pro** is enough for one cached daily pull ([Tokenomist](https://tokenomist.ai/pricing?plan=api-plan); [CryptoRank](https://docs.cryptorank.io/api-reference/vesting-&-token-unlocks/upcoming-token-unlocks)). Whale Alert, Arkham, LunarCrush and real-time Santiment cost a lot relative to their documented predictive value. They are low priority.

A starting weight scheme follows from the ranking. These weights are hypotheses for the ledger, not fitted values. Score every candidate on **beta-adjusted residual return**, so BTC moves are not credited to token signals. Apply hard vetoes ahead of any scoring: an upcoming team or insider unlock above a set share of float (for example, more than 1–2% of circulating supply within 30 days); an active Binance delisting or Monitoring Tag; a hack flag; an SEC naming. Within the remaining score, put roughly **35–40% on supply and notice state**, **20–25% on market regime and beta**, **15% on leverage crowding** (as a size cut, not a direction call), **10–15% on abnormal attention** (positive at mild z-scores, negative at extreme trending-top-3 or bot-heavy spikes), and **≤10% on adoption-per-value**. Give zero weight to partnerships, mainnets, airdrops and buyback headlines unless net supply after unlocks actually falls. Around FOMC and CPI hours, shrink size rather than taking a directional view.

Several hypotheses should go to the decision ledger for forward-testing, each logged with a timestamp, the detection latency against the official publish time, and BTC-adjusted returns at 1h, 24h, 7d and 30d. The first is a **delisting/Monitoring-Tag short or avoid rule** entered at detection, the cleanest edge for a slow poller. The second is an **Upbit-listing fade** entered after the peak, about an hour after detection, held to 24h or 7d. Upbit's 49-minute median time to peak suggests the fade window exists, but the 8-event live-capture sample is too small to trust ([cryptolisting.ws](https://cryptolisting.ws/listing-price-reaction/); [CoinSight](https://getcoinsight.com/en/research/new-listing-pump-by-exchange)). The third is a **pre-unlock avoid window** from day −30 to day +3, split by recipient type and by unlock size as a share of float, including Kim's unreplicated one-year-anniversary pattern. The fourth is **CoinGecko trending new-entrant continuation versus top-3 reversal**, which tests Santiment's 8.2% claim on independent data. The fifth is **OI-z-score and funding-extreme crash-magnitude conditioning**: does crowding predict the size of the next drawdown, which is what Oct 10 implies? Record every veto that fires as well, so the ledger can measure what the filters saved, not only what the triggers earned.

## Conclusion

The evidence reverses the usual narrative about altcoins. They are not mainly moved by what projects build or announce. They are moved by who is selling, through unlocks, market makers and delistings, and by how much leverage and BTC beta amplify that selling. Positive catalysts are mostly pre-priced. Negative supply and notice events are where measured, persistent and capturable effects sit, because their drift runs for hours to weeks rather than minutes. For Zobiris, the edge is defensive asymmetry more than prediction: avoiding the roughly −17% to −27% events reliably is worth more than chasing +14% pops it cannot reach first.

The biggest uncertainties are worth stating plainly. Most effect sizes since 2024 come from raw-return industry studies of a down-market. Partnership, mainnet, hack-alert lead times and small-cap macro responses are unmeasured. The size premium may not have survived the low-float era. The engine's own forward ledger, beta-adjusted and latency-stamped, will become better evidence for its niche than anything in this literature within a few months of running. It should be built to replace these priors, not confirm them.
